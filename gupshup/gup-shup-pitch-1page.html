Gup Shup

Kids build real Punjabi fluency by talking — in small groups of friends they already know, 15 minutes at a time.
The gap isn't vocabulary. It's peer-shame.
Kids need a room where speaking Punjabi with friends is normal — not another app that drills words they half-know.

MVP → Iteration → North Star

NOW · MVP

Prove kids want this

  • Invite-only pods, video call + human host
  • Topic cards, manual badges, no app needed
  • Pilot: 3–5 families + one Punjabi class
NEXT · ITERATION

Make it a product

  • Native app, scheduling, streaks, live badges
  • AI drafts topics + feedback; flags nudges for host
  • Cohorts get their own identity
LATER · NORTH STAR

Scale trust, not just tech

  • AI co-host handles live nudges
  • Vetted host marketplace, school partners
  • Expand beyond Punjabi to other mother tongues

Not Duolingo. Not Toastmasters.

Duolingo — solo drills, no one to talk to.
Toastmasters — social, but built for performance pressure. Assumes fluency.
Gup Shup — social and low-stakes: a playdate where Punjabi is the shared game.

Safety model at launch

Invite-only

Only peers a family already knows.

Adult present

A real host runs every session, live.

Effort > accuracy

Attempts rewarded before correctness.

The ask

Pilot Gup Shup this season with 3–5 families and one community Punjabi class.
