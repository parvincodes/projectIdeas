Two currencies, two jobs

🫙 Pod Points (shared)
  • Earned together, spent together
  • Unlocks pod-level rewards: team badge, special episode
  • Keeps the group cooperative, not competitive
👛 Personal Points (individual)
  • Earned by each kid for their own turns
  • Spent only in the Avatar Marketplace
  • Gives each kid autonomy & self-expression
