Three things I'm building

One for the body, one for the tradition, one for the tongue — each solving a problem I ran into with my own family first.

fitness

AI Fitness Coach

A coach that reads your training, sleep, and stress data daily, then adjusts the plan so you hit your goal without getting hurt. Marathon, swim, strength — you pick the goal, it bends the plan around a real, busy life, with a human coach approving the final call.

Why it's here too: not Sikh-specific, but built on the same principle Gurmat teaches about the body — steady, sustainable discipline over short bursts of intensity.

history

Gurmat research portal

A single study portal for Khalsa Gurmat School kids, built only on verified historical Sikh sources — not whatever the open internet says. A kid can ask a question in plain language and get an answer traced back to the actual text it came from.

Why it matters for Sikh families: Sikh history gets retold, flattened, and misquoted online more than most. Kids deserve a place that hands them the primary sources, not someone else's summary of them.

language

Gup Shup

A game, not an assignment: small groups of kids who already know each other talk in Punjabi for a timed, moderated session, and earn points toward avatars. Built because my own kids will speak Punjabi with me but switch to English with each other.

Why it matters for Sikh families: Punjabi isn't incidental to Sikhi — Gurbani lives in it. A kid who loses the spoken language loses a direct line to the Guru's word, not just a skill.
