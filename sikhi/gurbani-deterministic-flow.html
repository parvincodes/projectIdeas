Japji agent — the deterministic sandwich, end to end

Part 1 shows how the data is stored. Part 2 shows one question travelling through the system. Part 3 traces five real example prompts. Teal steps are deterministic, blue steps involve a model.

Part 1 · Build time: how the data and embeddings are stored

Scripture and teekas live in plain stores keyed by ID. Only an English gloss is embedded, and the index returns IDs, never text.

Part 2 · Query time: where the deterministic layer sits

D1 routes by rules, D2 returns verbatim records through MCP tools, D3 refuses to render anything it cannot trace to a stored record. Models only appear at ③ (candidate IDs) and ⑤ (a placeholder draft).

Part 3 · Example prompts, traced step by step

Step numbers match Part 2. Corpus facts (ਹਉਮੈ appears only in pauri 2; ਹੁਕਮ in pauris 1, 2, 3, 20, 27, 37; scholar coverage) come from the current skill files.

A
“explain pauri 5”
Reference lookup
2 · D1Regex matches pauri 5. Plan = {lookup:'ref', pauri:5}. P1 skipped.
4 · D2get_pauri(5) → lines from S1. get_interpretations(5) → rows from S2. coverage(5) → S3.
4 · D2 resultsahib_singh available:true · teja_singh available:true · manmohan_singh, sant_singh_khalsa available:false (not in corpus for pauri 5).
5 · P2Draft uses {{L:…}} for each line and {{I:sahib_singh:p05}}, {{I:teja_singh:p05}}. One sentence saying two readings are available.
6 · D3IDs resolve, scholars ∈ coverage. Substitutes verbatim text.
7 · KidGurmukhi, ang, two attributed readings, plus “Manmohan Singh and Dr. Khalsa: not available for this pauri”.
B
“where does Japji talk about ego?”
Open concept, hybrid
2 · D1No pauri/ang, no Gurmukhi. Plan = {lookup:'open'}. Synonym table adds ਹਉਮੈ / haumai.
3 · P1Embed question → top-k line IDs from S5 (gloss vectors).
4 · D2In parallel: find_literal(ਹਉਮੈ) → japji.p02.l06 (the only literal hit in the corpus). find_by_tag(ego) → same line. Merge by ID.
4 · D2 resultp02.l06 found_by [literal, tag] = exact. Any extra semantic IDs = possible. coverage(2) = sahib_singh, teja_singh, manmohan_singh.
5–6 · P2/D3Draft cites placeholders only; D3 verifies and fills.
7 · Kid“Exact word match: Pauri 2” + the line ਨਾਨਕ ਹੁਕਮੈ ਜੇ ਬੁਝੈ ਤ ਹਉਮੈ ਕਹੈ ਨ ਕੋਇ ॥੨॥ + three scholars. Semantic hits labelled “Related idea”.
C
“what does Dr. Khalsa say about pauri 2?”
Unavailable scholar
2 · D1Detects pauri 2 and scholar name sant_singh_khalsa. Plan = {lookup:'ref', pauri:2, scholar:'sant_singh_khalsa'}.
4 · D2get_interpretations(2, scholar='sant_singh_khalsa') consults S3: his coverage is [18].
4 · D2 result{available:false, reason:"not_in_corpus", available_scholars_for_this_pauri:["sahib_singh","teja_singh","manmohan_singh"]}
5 · P2Drafts: not available, offers the three that are. May not write any sentence attributed to Khalsa.
6 · D3Rejects any {{I:sant_singh_khalsa:p02}} placeholder (pair is unavailable). Retry or template.
7 · Kid“Dr. Khalsa’s reading isn’t in our sources for pauri 2. Here are three that are.”
D
“ਹੁਕਮੈ ਜੇ ਬੁਝੈ” (pasted Gurmukhi)
Literal Gurmukhi match
2 · D1Characters in U+0A00–0A7F → route = literal. Normalize to NFC first.
4 · D2find_literal() = substring scan of S1 → japji.p02.l06. No embeddings. No LLM.
5–6 · P2/D3Same placeholder draft; same verification.
7 · KidThe line, pauri 2, ang, and scholars. Label: exact match.
E
“where is hukam mentioned?”
Literal + tag
2 · D1Synonym table: hukam / hukm / ਹੁਕਮ. Plan = literal + tag.
4 · D2find_literal(ਹੁਕਮ) over S1 and find_by_tag(hukam) → pauris 1, 2, 3, 20, 27, 37.
7 · KidA list of pauris, each with the matching line. Marked exact. Note: pauri 2 is the one the Jarnail Singh essay discusses as Hukam.
